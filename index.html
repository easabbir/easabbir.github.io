01About

Quality engineering, from web apps to firmware

I’m a Staff SQA Engineer at Vivasoft Limited with more than 12 years in software quality assurance. I’ve tested food-aggregator, fintech, banking and ride-sharing products, on the web and on mobile.

My work covers manual and automated testing with Cypress, Playwright, Selenium and Appium, API testing with Postman, and performance testing with JMeter, K6 and Locust. More recently I’ve moved into embedded systems: integration-testing firmware on real IoT hardware with pytest over a serial CLI, wired into hardware CI.

I also mentor junior engineers and help teams build testing into their agile process, so releases ship with fewer surprises.

  • Test automation

    Cypress, Playwright, Selenium and Appium frameworks for web and mobile apps.

  • API & performance

    Postman and REST Assured for APIs; JMeter, K6 and Locust for load testing.

  • Embedded & firmware

    pytest suites on real IoT hardware over a serial CLI, running in hardware CI.

  • Team & process

    Mentoring QA engineers, agile delivery and UAT coordination.
